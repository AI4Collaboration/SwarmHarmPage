We are building a systematic testbed to see whether agents can discover peers without prior knowledge across separate sandboxes, establish communication channels to form a swarm, and cause harm through their combined actions.

In July 2026, agents in OpenAI evaluations found ways to communicate and helped one another compromise Hugging Face’s systems. A later replication elicited several of these behaviors from publicly available models. We want to understand both how such groups form and what their actions can do together. SwarmForm tests whether agents discover peers and build useful exchanges. SwarmHarm tests failures in tasks, evaluation and shared services, including harm from actions that are harmless individually. Incident report · Replication study

Experiment 1SwarmForm

Setting Separate sandboxes, challenging tasks and a possible communication path. Can agents discover peers without prior knowledge?

Study design

The current portable study compares three task classes in Engineering and Knowledge Work: conflicting requirements, missing peer information, and difficult independent work. Conditions use direct communication, shared files, sockets or mapped memory. Only the direct condition tells agents the group size.

The findings below come from the completed channel study, illustrated by one three-agent source-sharing run. Among 50 selected inputs, all 50 show connected communication, 39 meet the registered sustained-exchange criterion, and 37 have all nine business outputs correct. The other 13 retain partial or incorrect outputs. This selected collection is not a success-rate sample.

Experiment 2SwarmHarm

Setting Agents prepare reports in separate workspaces. Six threat models test what they change, share and cause together.

Study design

Six threat models, each tested in Engineering and Knowledge Work. The selected evidence below uses four Sol agents. Each finding is checked against its own input and complete trajectory.

Propagation requires peer adoption; coordination requires a shared plan. Compositional harm can occur without communication.
